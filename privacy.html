Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Same note as the Terms of Service: WOTAN is a community/hobby project, this policy has not been reviewed by a lawyer, and it's written to honestly describe what the site actually does today rather than to read like a template. It covers WOTAN (wotan.okemily.com) and IDUNA, the shared identity service WOTAN's sign-in runs through.

1. Information we collect

CategoryWhat it is
Account infoEmail address and display name, via IDUNA. If you sign in with Google, IDUNA receives your Google account identifier and basic profile info from Google; if you use an IDUNA email/password account, IDUNA stores your email and a salted password hash — never the plaintext password.
Game & social dataDecks you submit, posts/comments/likes on the Hearthstone feed, your public handle/profile, friends list and duel requests, match and leaderboard stats (e.g. SHANKPIT kills/deaths/sessions), and hat-store purchases (which cosmetic items you've bought/equipped with Flow).
Technical dataStandard web server logs (IP address, user agent, requested path, timestamp) kept for operating and securing the site, and IDUNA's own audit log of sign-in events.

We don't collect payment card data — WOTAN doesn't process real-money payments today (see the Terms of Service, "Flow and the hat store").

2. Cookies and local storage

Being precise about what WOTAN actually uses, rather than reusing boilerplate: WOTAN's own pages keep you signed in using your browser's local storage, not traditional HTTP cookies — your IDUNA session token and display name are stored there so every WOTAN page recognizes you without signing in again. IDUNA's own hosted sign-in and account pages (iam.okemily.com and related admin/portal pages) additionally set a small number of strictly-necessary, HttpOnly session cookies when you use them directly.

Either way, this data exists to keep you signed in and the site working — not for advertising. WOTAN does not currently use any third-party analytics or advertising trackers. The cookie banner shown on first visit covers both of these mechanisms under the umbrella term "cookies," since that's the common understanding of the phrase; dismissing it (or not) doesn't change what's stored for sign-in, since that's required for the site's core "stay signed in" feature to work at all. You can clear it anytime via your browser's site-data settings, which will sign you out.

3. How we use this information

  • To operate WOTAN's features: decks, profiles, feed, friends/duels, leaderboards, the hat store
  • To keep you signed in across WOTAN's pages and the other apps that share your IDUNA account
  • To show information you've chosen to make public (deck lists, handle, leaderboard position) to other visitors
  • To maintain security, investigate abuse, and enforce the Terms of Service

4. Sharing

We don't sell personal data, and we don't share it with advertisers. Because WOTAN uses one shared IDUNA account across the EINHORN_INDUSTRIAL ecosystem, your account info is necessarily visible to IDUNA and to whichever other EINHORN_INDUSTRIAL app you choose to sign into with it. If you sign in with Google, Google is involved in that authentication exchange per its own privacy policy — WOTAN/IDUNA only ever receive the basic profile fields needed to create your account.

5. Data retention and your rights

We keep account and gameplay data while your account is active. Being honest about the current state of this: there is no self-serve "export my data" or "delete my account" button yet, so a request to access, correct, or delete your data is handled manually — email us (below) and we'll act on it. This is a real limitation of a small community project, not a refusal; we intend to automate this as the site grows.

6. Children's privacy

WOTAN isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. See the Terms of Service for the account age requirement.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data (password hashing, ES256-signed session tokens, HttpOnly cookies where cookies are used at all), but no system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security. This hasn't gone through a formal third-party security audit or compliance certification.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site changes. Meaningful changes will update the "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact
