Deck tracker

Play Hearthstone as normal. Your cards left update as you draw. Only you can see this page’s data: it is tied to your IDUNA sign-in, and the uplink on your PC can only upload your own game.

Connect your PC

Open PowerShell on the PC that runs Hearthstone and paste this command. It opens your browser to sign in with IAM (the same login as this site and the EDGE.GAME client), then streams your game’s log lines (read-only) to your private tracker here. It signs in again by itself if the session expires.

Deck library

When a game ends, the deck you played is saved to your library with its tracked record. If you change a deck, the new version is saved as a copy; earlier versions are never edited.
