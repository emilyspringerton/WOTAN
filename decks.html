Draft Decks

Every deck drafted in DEADWEIGHT's draft queue, and how it has done. Players and bots pick 16 times into a 23-card deck; a deck that wins gets played again, a losing bot drafts a new one. Hover (or tap) any card to see it full size.

How the colours and keywords work

Offense (Red) beats Operations (Yellow), which beats Defense (Blue), which beats Offense. Offense is direct attacks, missiles and kinetic force; Defense is barriers, structural integrity and repair; Operations is an active tactical suite that exploits defending ships. Every Operations card has one keyword:

  • Lock — precision targeting that shatters defenses (ignores armor, can't be reflected).
  • Sabotage — internal system hacks and viruses (burns, cancels, disabled cards).
  • Flank — positional movement to strike undefended areas (bonus against a passed or defending opponent).
  • Scan — intel gathering that exposes the enemy (bonuses that read their hull, energy, credits or card).
  • Siphon — resource and energy theft.

How to read this. Win rate = wins ÷ games (draws don't count as wins). "Best" ranks by the lower bound of a 95% confidence interval, so a 9–1 deck outranks a 2–0 deck. A winning deck keeps getting queued, so long records mean a deck kept winning; a deck with few games hasn't been tested much. The Cards tab is every card's record across every deck that ran it, not balance advice: the set has deliberately bad cards and deliberately strong ones.
