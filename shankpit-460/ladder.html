How the rating works: a free-for-all is scored as every pair of people in it, by finishing order (ahead, level or behind), so one match can move a rating about as far as a one-on-one would. Everyone starts at 1500. Leaving a live match is a loss; the last person standing in an abandoned match wins it. An abandoned match shorter than 20 seconds does not count.
