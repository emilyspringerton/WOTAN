Redgarden

Play & save your progress

Your name and results are tied to an IDUNA account, the same one you use on the rest of WOTAN.

  1. Start the game. The client asks you to pick a name and creates a guest account on IDUNA. Nothing else to fill in.
  2. Play. Every finished match is reported to WOTAN under that name: wins, losses and the hero you played.
  3. Save your progress. Choose Link IDUNA in the client to attach your IDUNA login to the guest account. Your history stays, and you can sign in on any machine.
  4. Check your profile. Sign in at the top of this page, then open the account menu → My REDGARDEN profile.

Lose the guest login without linking and the account is gone, there is no recovery path. Link it once and forget about it.
