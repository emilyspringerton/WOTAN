An arena MOBA. Press play and you are matched into a 10v10 with the arena's resident bots: pick a hero, take the five nodes, hold them, and be the first side to 2000. Every finished match lands on your WOTAN profile.

Move & fightLeft or Right click moves, or locks onto and attacks the hero you click. A then click is attack-move, S stops. Hold the right button to keep steering. The wheel zooms.
AbilitiesQ W E are your three abilities, aimed at the pointer (W is a toggle for some heroes). ` uses a Blink Dagger or Donkey you own. H shows what your hero's abilities do.
The matchTake the five nodes and hold them: each one you own feeds your side's score, and the first side to 2000 wins the garden. Lane creeps march, towers shoot, jungle camps and their kings can be farmed. Tab shows the scoreboard.
ShopB opens it. Kills and creeps pay Flow; walk to your shop in your corner of the map, then press 1–9 or click to buy, Shift+1–5 for another page. Click a worn item to sell it back for half.
Guests & sign-inYou can play as a guest at once: IDUNA makes you a named account and this browser remembers it. Sign in with IDUNA to keep your record on your account and see it on any device.
What this isThe match is Red Garden's real arena server and its resident bot pool, the same game the native client plays. This page is a client for it: one WebAssembly module with no imports and no allocator, compiled from PARENA. The only JavaScript here is generated from PARENA too.

Playing the native client instead?
  1. Start the game. The first time, your browser opens to a short connect page. Pick a name, or sign in with the IDUNA account you already have.
  2. Save your progress (optional, 20 seconds). Right after picking a name you can add an email and password. That is what lets you sign in again on another computer.
  3. Play. Every finished match is reported under your name: wins, losses and the hero you played. The game remembers you next time, no browser needed.
  4. Check your profile. Sign in at the top of this page, then open the account menu → My REDGARDEN profile.

Skip the email step and the account lives only on that computer: lose the saved login and the account is gone, there is no recovery path.
