Redgarden

Play & save your progress

Your name and results are tied to an IDUNA account, the same one you use on the rest of WOTAN.

  1. Start the game. The first time, your browser opens to a short connect page. Pick a name, or sign in with the IDUNA account you already have.
  2. Save your progress (optional, 20 seconds). Right after picking a name you can add an email and password. That is what lets you sign in again on another computer.
  3. Play. Every finished match is reported under your name: wins, losses and the hero you played. The game remembers you next time, no browser needed.
  4. Check your profile. Sign in at the top of this page, then open the account menu → My REDGARDEN profile.

Skip the email step and the account lives only on that computer: lose the saved login and the account is gone, there is no recovery path.
