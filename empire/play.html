PlayPick a card from your hand (click it, or press 1–4), then click your half of the table. Troops go on your side of the river; spells anywhere. Esc or a right click puts the card back. Once an enemy princess tower falls you may also put troops down beside the river on that side.
ElixirTen at most, one every 2.8 seconds, twice as fast for the last minute. Every card costs some. A full bar is a quiet waste: it pulses gold.
StandingBIG_O's decorum, 0–100. A mythic card costs 25 of it (15 for the Goetia); a mundane one earns 1 back, and so does every few quiet seconds. Below 60 you are watched, below 30 frayed, and at 0 cancelled: no card can be played until it climbs back.
TowersThree minutes. A fallen king ends it at once; otherwise more crowns win. Level goes to one more minute where the first tower wins, and then to whoever has more tower health left.
The AIsPress play and, if nobody else is searching, one of eight AIs sits down with you in a few seconds. Choose one on the title screen to play it by name, or practise against any of them offline (nothing is recorded).
What this isThe whole game is one WebAssembly module with no imports and no allocator, compiled from PARENA. IDUNA runs the very same module as the referee and hosts the AIs; your copy applies the plays it is sent and draws them. The only JavaScript here that runs the game is generated from PARENA too.
