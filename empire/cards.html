Cards

Every card is one of ECOWAR's sixteen, with its hero and its base magnitude. The five mundane cards are quiet; the eleven mythic ones hit half as hard again and cost standing to cast — 25 of it, or 15 for the Goetia. At nothing, a faction is cancelled and cannot play a card at all. The numbers below are read from the game's own module when this site is built.
