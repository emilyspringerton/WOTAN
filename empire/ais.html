The AIs

Eight temperaments, each with a faction and a deck. They are part of the game itself, not a second implementation: the referee steps the same WebAssembly module you play in, and a bot asks the board the same questions you do (what do I hold, what is coming, what is it worth) and is bound by the same rules — elixir, standing, where it may place. They play each other in a league that anyone can watch.
