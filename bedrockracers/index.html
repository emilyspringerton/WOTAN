Bedrock Racers

Four teams of three rocket cars in one stadium. Carry the Quaffle with your magnet, pass it to a team-mate, and throw it up through one of the four upside-down goals. Two goals in a row and you are on fire.

DriveW S throttle and brake · A D steer · Space lift (hold it) · Shift boost · C fans off. Air is your fuel: lift and boost spend it, the road refills it, and nothing refills while you carry the ball.
Fly↓ pitches the nose up and ↑ down, like a flight stick pulled back · ← → roll · Q E strafe. Drive up a wall and over onto the ceiling: the camera turns the world with you.
The QuaffleHold F or the right mouse button near the ball: the magnet pulls it in and carries it. Let go to throw it along your nose, at your speed and then some. Hold it toward a team-mate who is holding their own magnet and the throw is a pass. Bump a carrier and the ball comes loose.
ScoringA ball that goes up into a shaft above the ceiling at one of the four corners is a goal. The bread-and-butter shot is a hard throw at a corner on the run: the ball climbs the curve and drops up into the tower. Be in on two of your team's goals in a row and you are on fire for 45 seconds.
MatchesPick your team and the bots' skill on the title (arrows, A D or click), then Enter to play or B to watch twelve bots. V ball cam · Tab scores · Esc pause. First to the goal limit or the clock, then a golden goal.
GamepadLeft stick steers and pitches · RT throttle, LT brake · A lift · B magnet · RB boost · LB fans off · right stick rolls and strafes · Start pauses.
What this isThe game is one WebAssembly module with no imports and no allocator, compiled from PARENA: the rocket-car physics, the bots and a software 3D renderer. The only JavaScript here is generated from PARENA too. It runs entirely in your browser: there is no sign-in, matchmaking or stat tracking yet.
