A one-on-one ship battler. Press play and you are matched with a bot, or with another person if one is waiting. You have sixty seconds to pack a ship, then thirty to fight with it. Every finished match is rated and lands on your WOTAN profile.

PackPick a part (click it, or its key: 1–9, 0, Q, W). Hover any part to read what it is for. R or the wheel turns it, and the ghost on the grid shows which way: a teal arrow is where energy comes in, a gold arrow where it goes out, a green dot a join and a hollow ring an open end. Click puts it on the 6×6 grid. Nothing is sent until you press Enter or time runs out, so move things freely: right click removes a part, Z undoes. The EXAMPLE button lays down a ship to learn from.
WireA generator makes 10 energy a second. Wires and splitters carry it (the gold dots are energy moving); an amplifier multiplies it by 1.5 for everything after it. A railgun fires every third second for 15, a pulse laser every second for 3 that skips armor, a siege cannon every fifth second for 30. A fed mender heals 2 hull a second, a fed aegis emitter refills 3 armor. A part with no energy says UNFED, and hovering it says why. Two feeds into one cell do nothing, and wire that loops back on itself burns out.
CargoTitanium Beam and Aether Ore are worth points of cargo, which decide a fight that ends level on hull. They can also be cut into smaller pieces: each piece needs room of its own and the old cells are ruined, but each piece leaves a dead square worth 8 armor (16 for the beam, 32 for the ore). Hover one and press C to see where its pieces would go; in the fight, 1–4 Panic Cuts one live.
FightThirty one-second ticks. The more hull you have at the end wins, or the one still standing. Both ships are shown the moment the fight starts, wired and working: shots fly across, the log says who hit whom and for how much, and hovering any part shows what it is doing. Every five seconds there is a round break.
Round breakA marker crosses a bar; press O (overcharge: harder shots, or a backfire if you miss) or B (brace: armor) as it meets the line. The line is placed for your connection's delay. When you are behind, a good press pays more.
What this isThe match is D2's real server and its bot pool, the same game the native client plays. This page is a client for it: one WebAssembly module with no imports and no allocator, compiled from PARENA. The only JavaScript here that runs the game is generated from PARENA too.
