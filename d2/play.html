A one-on-one ship battler. Press play and you are matched with a bot, or with another person if one is waiting. You have two minutes, 2618 credits and 105 tons to pack a ship, then thirty seconds to fight with it. Every finished match is rated and lands on your WOTAN profile.

PackPick a part (click it, or its key: 1–9, 0, Q–J); each tile shows its price. Two bars at the top are your budget: 2618 credits and 105 tons. There is no limit on how many parts you carry but the 36 cells, those two bars, and the clock. R or the wheel turns a part; the ghost shows which way: a teal arrow is where energy comes in, a gold arrow where it goes out. Right click removes a part, Z undoes, H shows the rules on one card. Nothing is sent until Enter or the clock runs low, so move things freely. EXAMPLE lays down a ship to learn from: the bot pool's own ships are in there.
LanesThe six columns of your hold are lanes. Fire comes down a lane from the bow (the top edge) and hits what the weapon hits: a pulse laser the first part, and only if nothing of yours stands in front of it; a railgun punches through, and what it did not spend carries on to the next part; a siege cannon is lobbed over everything onto the deepest part, and its blast hurts what touches it. The table under your hold says, lane by lane, how many shots of each it takes to open it. A lane with nothing in it is OPEN: shots go straight to the hull.
MaterialsEvery part has HP and is gone at zero (wires and guns too, so a shot can cut your circuit). A Bulwark takes half from lasers. Titanium takes half from slugs, and every gun touching it hits 1.5 times as hard. Aether Ore takes a fifth from lasers but is brittle to shells, and an Aegis Emitter touching it repairs faster. C splits a Titanium Beam into two Shards, or Ore into four Chips, right where it sits: the pieces cost and weigh more and boost less. Hover any part and every part it is tied to lights up.
Power and heatA generator makes 10 W; wires and splitters carry it (gold dots are energy moving). A gun fires once it has charged: laser 10, rail 30, cannon 30. A Quantum Core is 40 W in four cells but runs hot: every part touching it takes 2 a second unless a Cryo-Vent cools it. Xeno-Biomass grows back the parts it touches, an Aegis Emitter repairs the weakest part near it, a Mender mends the hull. A part with no energy says UNFED, and hovering it says why. Wire that loops back on itself burns out. Lighter ships dodge slugs and shells (never lasers).
FightThirty one-second ticks. The ships face each other bow to bow, yours below. Each second plays out across the second: a shot leaves its gun, runs down its lane, lands, and only then does the part flash, lose HP and send up a number. The hull is the wall at each ship's stern and slides down when hit, leaving a pale ghost of what it had. The log says who hit what with which weapon for how much, the gun list says what each is waiting for, and hovering any part shows what it is doing. The hull then the most structure left decides a fight that reaches thirty seconds.
Round breakEvery five seconds a marker crosses a bar; press O (overcharge: harder shots, or a backfire if you miss) or B (brace: a shield) as it meets the line. The line is placed for your connection's delay. When you are behind, a good press pays more.
What this isThe match is D2's real server and its bot pool, the same game the native client plays. This page is a client for it: one WebAssembly module with no imports and no allocator, compiled from PARENA. The only JavaScript here that runs the game is generated from PARENA too.
