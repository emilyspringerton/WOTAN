A one-on-one ship battler. Press play and you are matched with a bot, or with another person if one is waiting. You have sixty seconds to pack a ship, then thirty to fight with it. Every finished match is rated and lands on your WOTAN profile.

PackPick a part (1–7), R or the wheel turns it, click puts it on the 6×6 grid. Nothing is sent until you press Enter or time runs out, so move things freely: right click removes a part, Z undoes. The EXAMPLE button lays down a ship to learn from.
WireA generator makes 10 energy a second out of its east side. Wires and splitters carry it; a railgun that is fed fires every third second for 15 damage. Two feeds into one cell do nothing, and wire that loops back on itself burns out. The readout on the right says what your ship will do.
CutTitanium Beam and Aether Ore are cargo that can be cut into smaller pieces. Each piece needs room of its own, but the waste squares become armor. Hover a cargo piece and press C before the fight, or press 1–4 to Panic Cut one live.
FightThirty one-second ticks. The more hull you have at the end wins, or the one still standing. Your opponent's hold is sealed: you see only their hull and armor. Every five seconds there is a round break.
Round breakA marker crosses a bar; press O (overcharge: harder shots, or a backfire if you miss) or B (brace: armor) as it meets the line. The line is placed for your connection's delay. When you are behind, a good press pays more.
What this isThe match is D2's real server and its bot pool, the same game the native client plays. This page is a client for it: one WebAssembly module with no imports and no allocator, compiled from PARENA. The only JavaScript here that runs the game is generated from PARENA too.
