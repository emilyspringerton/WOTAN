WOTAN

The online social tournament site — decks, hats, player profiles, friends, and friendly-challenge duels.

Live

Player Profiles and Friends & Duels — public DEADWEIGHT player records, friend requests, and friendly-challenge duels. Sign in with the same IDUNA account as the Hat Store to manage friends and challenges — your DEADWEIGHT account needs an email linked first (from inside the DEADWEIGHT client) for IDUNA to recognize it here. An accepted duel mints a live match token either DEADWEIGHT client (native or browser) can queue with directly.

Draft Decks — every deck drafted in DEADWEIGHT's draft queue, with win rates and per-card records. Hover any card to see it full size. Open to everyone, no account needed.

Hearthstone Decks — the other half of the deck library: Hearthstone decks shared by players as readable lists and pasteable deck codes, with likes, comments, player profiles and a wall. A lighter, cream-and-gold section of its own; switch between it and DEADWEIGHT from the game switch in the top bar. Browsing is open to everyone.

Redgarden — the REDGARDEN (Knights of the Void) leaderboard, hero win rates and per-player profiles: heroes played and recent results. A third, garden-green section of its own with profiles separate from Hearthstone and DEADWEIGHT; switch from the game switch in the top bar. Browsing is open to everyone.

Matches — the real leaderboard (rating/W-L-D) and recent match list, plus a step-through replay of any match: every card played, every hull swing, replayed round by round through DEADWEIGHT's own match engine, cards shown as emoji by type. Open to everyone, no account needed.

Hat Store — the real Phase 2 store page (WOTAN_HAT_STORE_NORTHSTAR.md): log in with your WOTAN/IDUNA account, spend Flow on a BRAWLPIT cosmetic hat.

This page is now WOTAN's own real home. The REDGARDEN bot leaderboard, hero-strength stats, and the in-browser GFD Battlegrounds demo haven't moved yet and are still at okemily.com/tournaments.html — that page isn't going away, it just isn't the front door anymore.
